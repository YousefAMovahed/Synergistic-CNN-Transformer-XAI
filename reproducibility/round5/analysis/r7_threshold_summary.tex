\paragraph{Threshold}
Over operating thresholds from 0.50 to 0.95 on the five re-runs, the net gain
was positive at every threshold up to 0.90 in the four seeds with gains and
peaked between 0.70 and 0.85, whereas the gain in balanced accuracy was largest
between 0.55 and 0.70; in seed~0 every threshold lost predictions. The archived
value 0.6585 is therefore not the accuracy maximum of the test set
(Section~\ref{sec:supp_robust}, Figure~\ref{fig:sweep}).
